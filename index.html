    04 — About

    Design-minded
    engineering.

    I'm Zaeed, a full-stack developer in Zamboanga City. Over the past 3+ years I've built web systems and business sites for schools and small organizations — internal reporting tools, request desks, capstone platforms, and custom apps — mostly with Laravel, MySQL, and Tailwind.

    I also work as a social media associate, managing Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok pages: content planning, posting schedules, and performance monitoring. That side of the work shapes how I build — I think about how people find, read, and use a product, not only how it is coded.

    I graduated with a BS in Information Technology from Western Mindanao State University in 2024 and am now pursuing a Master of Information Technology there. I use AI-assisted tools like Claude and Qoder to move faster, while keeping data modeling, permissions, testing, and handover fully reviewed. Sometimes I call that vibe coding; the review and testing are what keep it useful.

    Functional first. Accessible by default. Built to be maintained.
