Focus
Web systems & platforms

I build functional, user-friendly web applications — capstone and thesis systems, custom web-based systems for small businesses, and the platforms that hold them together. I care about how a system works after launch: clear structure, clean records, and a handover a client can actually follow.

Beyond development, I work as a social media associate — managing and monitoring Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. That means content ideas, posting strategies, performance monitoring, and finding the opportunities that improve audience engagement and online visibility.

More than visually appealing — functional, accessible, and designed around the end user.

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Western Mindanao State University in 2024 and am currently pursuing a Master of Information Technology at the same university. I use AI-assisted tools and vibe coding to move faster, while keeping the output reviewed, tested, and maintainable.
